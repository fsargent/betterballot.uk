This is the “Sandbox Mode” for To Build a Better Ballot (UK). If you haven't already played it, read the whole thing here!

Make your own single-winner voting simulation: drag the candidates and voters, switch between FPTP / Choose one, the Alternative Vote and Choose many, and hit “save” to get a shareable link.

Want proportional methods — Party List, STV and SPAV? Those live in the multi-member simulator over in Part 3 of the main piece.
